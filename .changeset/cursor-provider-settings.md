Cursor is now a provider. Turn it on in Settings > Providers > Cursor, sign in with your Cursor account from the settings page (the sign-in link opens on your device even when the environment runs on another machine), and pick a Cursor model for a bot. Cursor bots are read-only unless you choose Full access, run only in the Local sandbox, and report usage under Cursor.
