Group chats now let a bot hand the conversation to another member by mentioning it in a reply. Each chat has its own reply budget, from 1 to 20 and 6 by default, that caps how many replies a chain of mentions adds for one message. Bots you name yourself always answer, even past the budget, and a chain the budget cuts short is recorded so clients can say so.
